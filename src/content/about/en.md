I work mostly on the part nobody sees: data models, APIs, authentication and
permissions. Almost every project I have built started with an awkward
requirement behind it, and that is the part I find interesting. An invoicing
system is worthless if the tax authority rejects the receipt. A clinical system
is worthless if the wrong professional can open a patient record.

I have led small teams on two of those projects: assigning tasks, reviewing my
teammates' code and validating every feature before signing it off. What I
learned is that the hard part of coordinating is not splitting the work, but
holding a shared standard when everyone solves their own module their own way.

I am drawn to enterprise systems, infrastructure and security. Right now I am
looking for an internship where those problems are the daily work rather than a
course assignment.
